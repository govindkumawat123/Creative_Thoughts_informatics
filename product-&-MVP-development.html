Start With the Features That
Matter Most

An MVP, or Minimum Viable Product, is a working version of a product that includes the essential features needed to solve a core user problem. Instead of investing in every possible feature at the beginning, an MVP helps you test your assumptions, understand user needs, and make better decisions about future development.

For example, an appointment-booking MVP might include:

User registration
Service discovery
Availability and booking
Booking confirmation
Basic account management

Advanced loyalty programs, complex analytics, and additional automation could be introduced later if user needs justify them.

The goal is simple: reduce the initial scope, not the quality of the product.

Keep the core experience usable, secure, and reliable while focusing development effort on the features that matter most.
