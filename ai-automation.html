DOES YOUR
BUSINESS NEED
AI OR TRADITIONAL
AUTOMATION?

Traditional automation works well when tasks follow clear, predefined rules. AI automation is useful when a process also requires understanding text, documents, customer requests, or less-structured information.

TRADITIONAL AUTOMATION

When an order is paid, update its status and send a confirmation email.

AI AUTOMATION

Understand a customer's written request, retrieve relevant information, and direct it to the appropriate workflow

Many business processes benefit from a combination of AI, traditional automation, APIs, and human review.
